The Easy Way to Get IKEA Home

IKEA Reading is a 40-minute drive from Oxford — but the route home is brutal if your boot won't close. We run pickups from IKEA Pingle Drive (RG2) daily, delivering to Oxford, Abingdon, Bicester, Witney, Didcot, Headington and surrounding postcodes. Same-day if you book before 11am.

IKEA's own home delivery starts around £45 and takes 1-3 days with a 4-hour window. Our IKEA delivery is from £60-£180 depending on volume and distance, often same-day, with a 1-hour delivery slot. For one wardrobe and a desk, often cheaper. For a flat-load haul, much faster.

How It Works

1. Buy at IKEA Reading. Pay in store, get your order receipt. Tell staff you're using your own delivery — they'll hold items at customer collection.

2. Send us a quote request. Photo of receipt or item list, your Oxfordshire address, and preferred delivery time. We come back with a fixed price within the hour.

3. We collect & deliver. Our driver picks up at IKEA's customer collection point and delivers direct to your door.

4. You pay on delivery. Bank transfer, cash, or card. No deposit needed for same-day jobs under £200.
