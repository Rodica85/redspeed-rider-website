Student Move FAQ

How early should I book? Two weeks ahead for term-end (Trinity especially), one week for any other time. Same-week slots often available outside June.

Do you cover all colleges? All Oxford colleges, Brookes campuses, plus surrounding student houses in Cowley, Headington, East Oxford and Jericho. Outside Oxford too — Bath Spa, Reading Uni, Coventry, anywhere UK.

Can I split the cost with flatmates? Yes — we'll quote one trip with multiple stops. Cheaper than three separate jobs.

What if my move date changes? Free reschedule up to 24h before. Term dates change, exam timetables shift — we get it.
