Furniture Collection, Delivery & Assembly

Furniture delivery doesn't have to be expensive or complicated. Whether you've ordered a flat-pack wardrobe from IKEA, a sofa from DFS, or spotted a vintage dresser on Facebook Marketplace — REDSPEED RIDER will collect it, deliver it to your door, carry it upstairs, and if you like, put it together for you.

We operate a large van that fits everything from a single bedside table to a full three-piece suite. Same-day service is often available across Oxfordshire.

Where We Collect From

Regular pickups from all major UK retailers and marketplaces.

IKEA

Wembley, Reading, Milton Keynes or further. We'll pick up your order and deliver same-day where possible.

DFS, Sofology, Oak Furnitureland

Sofas, dining sets, wardrobes — collected from store or warehouse and carefully delivered.

Argos, B&Q, Wickes

Flat-pack or assembled — collected from your local store and delivered straight to your home.
