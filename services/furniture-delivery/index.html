Furniture Collection & Delivery

Furniture delivery doesn't have to be expensive or complicated. Whether you've ordered a flat-pack wardrobe from IKEA, a sofa from DFS, or spotted a vintage dresser on Facebook Marketplace — REDSPEED RIDER will collect it from the seller or store and deliver it safely to your door.

We operate a large van that fits everything from a single bedside table to a full three-piece suite. Same-day service is often available across Oxfordshire.
