Business Moves, Minimised Downtime

Every hour your office is offline costs your business. REDSPEED RIDER plans around that. We do most office moves at evenings, weekends or overnight so you can close on Friday and reopen Monday with everything in place.

We handle desks, chairs, filing cabinets, IT equipment (carefully wrapped), printers, meeting tables — and can dismantle and reassemble flat-pack furniture on arrival. For small offices (up to ~15 people), one van and one long day is usually enough.

What We Handle

IT Equipment

Monitors, PCs, servers — wrapped in anti-static blankets and secured in transit.

Desks & Chairs

Office chairs, sit-stand desks, meeting tables. Dismantled and reassembled where needed.

Files & Archives

Secure transport of filing cabinets and document boxes. Chain-of-custody if required.

Printers & Copiers

Large multi-function machines handled carefully. We'll work with your provider if needed.

Out-of-Hours

Evening, weekend and overnight moves available. Minimise impact on your working week.

Fully Insured

Goods-in-transit insurance covers your office assets from pickup to setup.
