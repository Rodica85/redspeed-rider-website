Things change fast. The buyer wants the sofa collected before they reconsider. The flat handover is at 4pm. The new tenant moves in tomorrow but the old one's bed is still there. Same-Day FAQ

How fast can you pick up? Local Oxfordshire jobs: usually within 1-3 hours of booking. We'll give you a window before you commit.

Is same-day more expensive? Slight premium (£10-£30) over a non-urgent booking: but cheaper than losing the sale or missing your handover.

Do you do nights / Sundays? Yes, we operate 7am-9pm Monday-Saturday and 8am-8pm Sunday. Outside those hours, call anyway: we'll often help.

Do you cover long-distance same-day? Yes, Oxford to London, Birmingham, Bristol, Cambridge, Reading, all done same-day if booked before 1pm. Beyond that, next-day guaranteed.

How do I pay urgently? Bank transfer (instant), card via Stripe link, or cash on delivery. No deposit required for same-day jobs under £200.
