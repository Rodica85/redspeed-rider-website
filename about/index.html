About REDSPEED RIDER LTD

A local man-with-a-van service that grew into Oxfordshire's go-to removals team. Same driver, same care, same promise on every job.

Who We Are

REDSPEED RIDER LTD is a fully registered UK limited company based in Abingdon, Oxfordshire. What started as a single van helping neighbours move furniture has grown into a proper removals service: trusted by students moving into Oxford halls, families relocating across the country, small businesses running weekly pallet deliveries, and market traders setting up every weekend.

We're not a big national franchise. We're a small, responsive local team: which means when you call, you speak to the person who'll actually be doing your move. No call centres, no sub-contractors you've never met.

What We Stand For

Fair Prices

Fixed quotes, no hidden fees, no "oh you didn't mention that" upsells. What we quote is what you pay.

On Time

We arrive when we say we will. Your time is as valuable as ours: we build realistic schedules around it.

Care for Your Stuff

Your belongings matter. We bring blankets, straps and an old-school sense of responsibility to every load.

Clear Communication

You'll hear from us when we're on the way, at pickup, and at drop-off. No silence, no guessing.

Properly Insured

Full goods-in-transit cover on every single job: not an optional extra.

Responsible Disposal

Old furniture, waste, clearance: disposed of at licensed facilities, not fly-tipped.
