Quick Contact FAQ

How fast will you reply? Within an hour during working times — usually within 15 minutes on WhatsApp. Out of hours we read messages first thing the next morning.

Do I need to pay anything to get a quote? No. Quotes are always free, written, and binding for 14 days. No deposit needed to confirm a same-week booking.

Can I get a fixed price rather than hourly? Yes — we prefer fixed quotes. Tell us pickup, drop-off, item list (or photos) and we'll come back with one all-in number. No "extra time" charges.

What payment methods do you take? Bank transfer (preferred), cash on completion, or card via Stripe link. Business accounts can be invoiced with 14-day terms after the first job.

Do you cover outside Oxfordshire? Yes — we run UK-wide regularly. Long-distance moves are typically same-day for one-van loads. Ask for a quote with both postcodes.
