Frequently Asked Questions

The things people ask most before booking. Can't find your answer? Just call or WhatsApp — we're happy to chat.

How much does a man with a van cost in Oxford?

A typical short pickup within Oxford or Abingdon costs £40–£60. A full 1-bed flat move is £200–£350. Student moves usually land between £80–£120. Every job is quoted individually — see our pricing page for detailed examples.

How do I get a quote?

Three ways: (1) fill out the form on our homepage, (2) WhatsApp us at 07424 714686 with pickup and drop-off postcodes plus a description, or (3) call direct on +44 7424 714686. We usually respond within an hour during working times.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry goods-in-transit insurance covering your belongings from pickup to delivery. We're also a fully registered UK limited company — REDSPEED RIDER LTD.

How much notice do I need to give?

For small jobs and pickups, same-day or next-day service is often available. For full house moves — especially end-of-month or weekend dates — we recommend booking at least 1–2 weeks ahead.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Abingdon, Oxfordshire (OX14), and cover Oxford, Didcot, Witney, Bicester and all surrounding villages, as well as regular runs to London, Reading and across the UK. See our area pages for details.

Can you help with packing?

Yes — full or partial packing service is available as an add-on. We bring boxes, tape, bubble wrap and protective blankets. Let us know when requesting a quote and we'll include it in the price.

Do you take payment in advance?

For most jobs we take payment on completion — cash, bank transfer or card. For very long-distance or business account work, we may request a deposit. You'll always know the total upfront.

What if there are stairs at my flat?

We include ground-floor access and one flight of stairs in our standard quote. Higher floors, narrow staircases or lift restrictions add a small surcharge — we always tell you up front, never as a surprise on moving day.

Can you assemble furniture on arrival?

Yes — flat-pack assembly is a popular add-on, especially for IKEA wardrobes, beds and desks. Add a note when you request a quote so we allow the time.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes — we operate 7 days a week, including evenings. Saturday is our busiest day for house moves, so book ahead.

What can't you move?

We don't move hazardous materials (fuel, chemicals, flammables), live animals, or anything illegal. Very large items like pianos or safes need specific advance discussion — we can often do it but will need to assess.

Can I get an invoice for my business?

Yes — we provide VAT-compliant invoices on request. For regular business customers we offer monthly account invoicing.

Still Have a Question?

Just call, WhatsApp or email us. We're happy to answer anything before you book.
