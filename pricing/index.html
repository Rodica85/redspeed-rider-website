Transparent Man with a Van Pricing

No hourly surprises. No hidden fees. Here's exactly how we quote every job — so you know before you book.

Fixed Price, Agreed Up Front

We don't charge by the hour. Every job gets a fixed quote based on four simple factors — distance, van size needed, time required, and anything extra (heavy items, longer routes, multiple stops). Once we agree the price, that's what you pay. No fuel surcharges, no "more men" fees, no tip expectations.

What Goes Into Your Quote

1. Distance

Pickup postcode → drop-off postcode. Short local jobs cost less; cross-country runs more.

2. Van Size & Load

Most jobs fit in our large van in one trip. Bigger moves may need two trips.

3. Time Required

Loading, driving, unloading — quick pickups are cheaper than full-day removals.

4. Extras

Heavy items, multiple stops, evenings or weekends — all priced clearly upfront.
