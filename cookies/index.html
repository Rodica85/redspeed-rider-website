Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 28 April 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a website. They help websites work properly, remember your preferences, and (sometimes) measure how the site is used.

2. Cookies We Use

We deliberately keep cookie usage minimal. Our website uses only the following:

Essential Cookies (always active)

These are strictly necessary for the website to work. They cannot be turned off.

Cookie Set by Purpose Lifetime
__cf_bm Cloudflare Bot management: keeps the site safe from automated abuse 30 minutes
cf_clearance Cloudflare Confirms you are a human visitor (no challenge needed) 1 year
rsr_cookie_consent Our site Remembers your cookie banner choice 1 year

Analytics Cookies (optional, only if you accept)

If you click "Accept all" on the cookie banner, we may set the following:

Cookie Set by Purpose Lifetime
_clck, _clsk Microsoft Clarity Anonymous analytics: heatmaps and session insights to improve the site 1 year / 1 day

If you decline, no analytics cookies are set.

3. Third-Party Resources (No Cookies)

Our site loads fonts and icons from CDN providers. These do not set cookies but transmit your IP for delivery:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com): Inter font family
  • Cloudflare CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): Font Awesome icons

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control cookies in three ways:

  1. Our cookie banner: choose "Accept all", "Decline non-essential" or "Manage preferences" on first visit. Click here to change your preferences at any time.
  2. Your browser settings: all major browsers let you block or delete cookies. See aboutcookies.org for instructions.
  3. Browser privacy mode: Incognito / Private window blocks most third-party cookies automatically.

5. Do Not Track

Our website does not use cross-site tracking. We do not have advertising cookies, social media trackers, or remarketing pixels. The most we ever do is anonymous analytics: and only with your explicit consent.

6. Updates to This Policy

If we add new cookies (for example, adding analytics in the future), we'll update this page and notify you via the cookie banner.
